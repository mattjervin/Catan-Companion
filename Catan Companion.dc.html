Rules refresher

{{ rulesKicker }}

Turn order

Roll → everyone collects production → trade → build. Trade and build in any order, as often as you can afford.

Rolling a 7

Everyone holding 8+ cards discards half, rounded down. The active player moves the robber and steals 1 random card from an adjacent opponent.

Building costs

Road — brick + wood · Settlement — brick + wood + wheat + sheep · City — 2 wheat + 3 ore · Development card — wheat + sheep + ore.

Trading rates

4:1 with the bank any time. 3:1 at a generic harbor you've settled, 2:1 at a matching-resource harbor. Player trades are unrestricted.

Opening placement

Settlement + road in turn order, then a second pair in reverse order. The second settlement collects one resource from every adjacent hex.

Special cards

Longest Road — 5+ unbroken segments, 2 VP. Largest Army — first to play 3 Knights, 2 VP. Both pass to whoever beats them.

Development deck — 25

14 Knight · 5 Victory Point · 2 Road Building · 2 Year of Plenty · 2 Monopoly. Only a VP card may be played the turn it's bought.

Winning

Race to {{ vpTarget }} VP and declare on your own turn. Settlement 1 · City 2 · Longest Road 2 · Largest Army 2 · VP cards 1 each.

Seafarers adds

Building ships

1 wool + 1 lumber. Any hex edge where at least one side is water, extending from your settlement/city or another of your ships. Branching allowed.

Moving a ship

A route open at one end may move its outermost ship — once per turn, never one built that same turn. Routes closed at both ends are locked.

Roads meet ships

Roads and ships never join directly — a settlement or city must sit at the junction to count as one continuous route.

Longest trade route

Replaces Longest Road, counting roads and ships together. Moving a ship doesn't cost you the card if the route still qualifies.

The pirate ship

On a 7 move the pirate instead of the robber, to any sea hex. No ship may be built on or moved off its borders; steal 1 card from a player with an adjacent ship.

Gold fields

Produce on their number like any hex, but the owner picks what each card becomes. A city on gold picks two.

New-island bonus

The first settlement any player lands on a discovery island earns a special VP token — one per player, never lost once earned.

Is the map random?

Half and half. The scenario fixes island shapes, sea gaps and harbor spots; terrain and numbers inside them are shuffled — exactly what the dealer above does.

Cities & Knights

Same island — harder life. Play to 13 VP.

Add to the table
Barbarian tile + black ship on its start space
3 metropolis pieces and the trader cone
Yellow + red number dice and the event die
Commodity stacks face up: paper · cloth · coin
Progress decks shuffled separately by flag colour
Knights, city walls and improvement boards per player
Victory target
13 VP

Metropolis 4 VP each (a plain city is 2) · Defender of Catan 1 VP each · plus VP progress cards.

Roll all three dice

Resolve the event die first, before any resources are handed out. Yellow + red then drive production as normal.

Event die — ship

Three faces show a ship: the barbarian fleet advances one space along its track. When it lands, the attack resolves.

Event die — gate

The other three faces are coloured city gates. Players whose improvement level in that colour meets the red die draw that type of progress card.

Commodities

Cities — never settlements — on forest, mountain and pasture pay 1 resource + 1 commodity (paper · coin · cloth). Fields and hills cities still pay 2 resources.

City improvements

Three tracks of five levels — Politics (paper) · Trade (cloth) · Science (coin). Level 3 unlocks a special ability; level 4 unlocks a metropolis.

Metropolis

First to level 4 on a track flips one city to that metropolis. One per track — a rival who climbs higher takes it. Barbarians can never destroy one.

Knights

Hire for 1 sheep + 1 ore on an intersection along your roads. Activate with 1 wheat. Promote basic → strong → mighty for 1 sheep + 1 ore each. Two of each per player.

Barbarian attack

Attack strength = every city and metropolis on the board. Defence = combined strength of all active knights. Lose and the weakest contributor drops a city; win and the top contributor takes Defender of Catan.

City walls

2 brick each, one per city, three per player. Every wall raises your 7-discard limit by 2 — up to 13 cards. No protection from robber or barbarians.

The robber, rethought

It moves only on a Bishop progress card or a failed barbarian defence — not on every 7. You still discard on a 7, and an active knight can chase it off for free.

Progress cards

Three decks — Politics (yellow) · Trade (green) · Science (blue). They replace the base development deck entirely. Hold 4 between turns, 5 during your own.

Stacked with Seafarers

The two combine. Gold fields pay resources only — never commodities — and a shipping route counts as closed at a knight as well as a settlement.
Built for a printed magnetic set

Land hexes are dealt fresh each roll — use the board for placement and the panels for how many of each tile to pull. Seafarers component counts vary slightly by edition; cross-check the scenario card in your box.
